ABOUT DINE & SEEK

Discover More.

Dine & Seek is a restaurant explorer designed to help users discover different restaurants and find places that match what they are looking for.

OUR PURPOSE

Making Restaurant Discovery Easier

Finding a restaurant can sometimes be difficult when there are many choices available. Dine & Seek makes the process easier by organizing restaurant information in one place.

Users can explore restaurants, check their cuisine, location, health grades, and scores, then choose a place that fits their preferences.
